How it works

  1. 01

    Add a photo.

    It fills the front. Pan, zoom and straighten it. What you set is what prints.

  2. 02

    Pick a handwriting.

    Use one of our styles, upload a font, or trace your own from a photo.

  3. 03

    Write the note.

    Type it. The pen adds a little wobble and uneven spacing, so no two cards come out exactly alike.

  4. 04

    Address it.

    Add their address, and yours if you want it on the back. Check it, then send it.

Use your own handwriting.

Write a sentence that uses every letter of the alphabet, once normally and once in capitals, and photograph each. We find the letters and use them to write your cards, filling in any the pages missed.
