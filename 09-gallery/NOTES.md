# 09 Gallery Wall

**Concept.** A quiet museum: the postcard hangs on a pale concrete wall as a framed work with a spotlight, a real shadow and a catalogue label, and the page is a walk through four rooms (the photo, the pen, your handwriting, the price). The only colour is ultramarine, which appears as the pen ink, the letter-detection boxes, focus rings and the active room on the floor plan.

**Palette.** Wall `#f4f3ef`, rooms `#ebeae5` / `#e7e6e1`, floor `#dcdbd5`, paper `#fbfaf6`, ink `#171716` (text 2/3: `#3d3c38`, `#5b5a54`), pigment ultramarine `#2a3b92`. Sharp corners everywhere except the doorway arches.
**Fonts.** Familjen Grotesk (UI, headlines), Cormorant Garamond italic (wall text and labels only), Reenie Beanie (only for the sample handwritten sheets in Room III, to stand in for a person's writing).

**Signature moment.** The pinned horizontal walk (`.walk`): one scrubbed `gsap.timeline` on a ScrollTrigger (`pin`, `scrub: 1.2`, bounded to about 5 viewport heights). `xPercent` slides the track between rooms with `power2.inOut`; each arriving room opens through an arched `clip-path` doorway while a dim "corridor" overlay pulses and its spotlight cone fades up. In Room II a proxy tween drives all 40 real plotted paths at constant pen speed (stroke-dashoffset), moves a drawn pen along the path, and the wall label crossfades "Card, back / Plotting / Wish you were here" with a live "Stroke n of 40". Room I straightens and zooms the photo with a live pan/zoom/straighten readout; Room III scans letter boxes across sample sheets via a tweened CSS variable. Elsewhere: SplitText masked H1, line-by-line wall text (clip-path), ScrollTrigger.batch reveals, Flip-driven FAQ accordion, ScrollToPlugin anchors, a hanging-frame sway. Below 1024px wide (or under 620px tall) the rooms stack vertically with no pin and each gets its own scrubbed moment. Reduced motion and no-JS show everything, note already written.

**A real port would still need.** Dark mode (the concept is light-only; the card and concrete would need an evening-gallery variant), the other pages, real photographs of mailed cards (the coast photo, note and sheets are SVG/CSS samples and say so), a real form-free `Make one` flow, hash-on-load handling for `#pricing` while pinned, and the 4 x 6 in. size confirmed with Henry (taken from the direction text, not PRODUCT.md).

**Known flaws.** The pen-tip and note artwork is tuned for 1440x900; at very short desktop heights the art shrinks and the note gets small. Room III uses a web font as a stand-in for handwriting. The HUD floor plan only exists in the pinned mode. FAQ closing is instant for the collapsing panel (Flip slides the rows after it). Google Fonts need a network connection; fallbacks are Helvetica/Garamond/cursive.
